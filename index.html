03 / Featured 3D Projects

Blender 3D Projects & Animations

Two complete 3D animated scenes modeled, textured, lit, and rendered in Blender — showcasing material realism, lighting atmosphere, and camera storytelling.

🎬 Project 01 • 3D Animation & Shading

"The Diner's Blend" — Café Tabletop Scene

A stylized photorealistic café scene featuring an iced pink donut with scattered sprinkles sliding beside a handcrafted two-tone wood & ceramic coffee mug against an exposed brick backdrop, complete with ambient café audio and title typography.

  • Modeling & Sculpting: Subdivision surface modeling, organic icing drip sculpting, and procedural sprinkle distribution.
  • Materials & PBR Texturing: Rough stone countertop, normal-mapped brick wall, glossy ceramic plates, and wood-grain mug shaders.
  • Animation & Post: Keyframed plate slide with realistic deceleration, shallow depth-of-field camera, title overlay, and audio mix.
  • Blender 3D
  • Subsurf & Sculpting
  • PBR Shaders
  • Keyframe Animation
  • Cycles / EEVEE
🌆 Project 02 • Interior & Lighting Study

"Golden Hour Window" — Interior & Urban Vista

A moody golden-hour architectural interior scene looking past a set marble dining table toward a silhouetted cat perched on the window ledge overlooking warm sunlit high-rise buildings, captured with a smooth cinematic camera push-in.

  • Scene Composition: Layered foreground-to-background depth with dining table props (succulent planter, cutlery holder, woven placemats) framing the window silhouette.
  • Lighting & Atmosphere: Warm sunset directional lighting casting soft interior shadows alongside fabric curtain translucency and polished marble reflections.
  • Camera & Motion: Smooth forward camera dolly with shallow depth-of-field rack focus and subtle secondary character motion on the window cat.
  • Interior Visualization
  • Golden Hour Lighting
  • Camera Dolly & DoF
  • Marble & Fabric Shaders
  • Storytelling
